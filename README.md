# project-taekwon-do
Student profiles featuring their backgrounds, highlights, support methods, and sports-related promotional material—a project for Dojang Ko-Dang, the school where I practice ITF Taekwon-Do.

## Description 
This page is designed to provide information about Dojang Ko-Dang, an ITF Taekwondo school. Here, you will find everything you need to know to join, including the school's history and growth, as well as individual profiles of Dojang Ko-Dang students—featuring their highlights, medals, certifications, and personal biographies—and ways to support them. It was created to promote the school within a modern, adaptable, and easily updatable environment.

The site features a single interface divided into seven sections. 
The first is, of course, the main page, where you can find the school's name and logo, the Taekwondo methodology taught, the address, direct contact details, and shortcuts for quick access to specific sections (an interactive menu at the top functions in the same way).

In the second section, you can learn about our school—starting with the history behind what it has become today—and watch videos of our activities while you read.

The third section focuses on the school's instructors—those who impart knowledge—and the guiding figure they follow in doing so; it includes their personal profiles and photos, allowing you to get to know them individually.

The fourth section features all the school's practitioners, categorized by color ranks and black belt ranks—organized both hierarchically and alphabetically—and includes photos, names, ages, and brief biographical summaries for each.
Each profile is clickable and opens a dedicated interface where you can view your favorite student—including highlights, certificates, medals, ways to support them individually, full name, weight, and other details.

The fifth section covers all the awards Ko-dang has won as a school, or those earned by the instructors in charge of leading it.

In the sixth section, you can view all the details on how to join the academy and start training, including schedules and the exact address.

The final section is aimed at sponsors or brands interested in the school and its students, as associating with high-level athletes can generate visibility and popularity.
